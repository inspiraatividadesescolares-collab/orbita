Handle occupies the upper quarter; body occupies the lower three quarters; the broad front panel is centered approximately at 65 percent of image height. Soft studio light from upper left, sophisticated gradients and lovely realistic metallic highlights, gently rounded edges, tasteful stylized 3D rendering consistent with a colorful educational platform. Constraints: exactly ONE isolated weight; no other objects, no scene, no floor, no background shadow; no letters, no numerals, no kg, no text, no logo, no watermark, no painted checkerboard.
